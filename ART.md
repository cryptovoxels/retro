# Art

We show art from the Ethereum, Polygon, and Base chains. Copy the URL for your piece from OpenSea, or paste a CAIP-19 id if you already have one.

## how to hang it

0. Add an NFT image to your parcel
1. Open the piece on [OpenSea](https://opensea.io)
2. Copy the item URL from the address bar (it looks like `/item/ethereum/...`)
3. Select the NFT image, open the URL tab, paste
4. Got a CAIP-19 id from a wallet or another tool? Paste that instead. Same wall.

## Chain Agnosticism

CAIP-19 is the [cross-chain standard](https://standards.chainagnostic.org/CAIPs/caip-19) for linking to NFTs. OpenSea URLs also work. We keep whatever you pasted.
