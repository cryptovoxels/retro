# How to build in Voxels (again)

New here, or been away long enough that the keys feel rusty? This is the short version. Two links, then you can ignore us and go make something.

## Practice in a sandbox

Open [/build](/build) and pick a sandbox. Anyone can edit there. The learn checklist walks you through it when you arrive with `learn=true`.

If you skip that, the muscle memory is:

1. **B** turns on the voxel tool. Mouse locks to the world. **Escape** frees it.
2. **Click** to place a block. **Drag** for a wall or a floor.
3. **Shift-click** (or shift-drag) to delete.
4. Pick a color on the toolbelt, or press **1-9** while you are building.
5. **Ctrl-click** to paint a block with that color.

**Tab**, **+** on the toolbelt, or **Add** in the menu lets you peek at signs, images, video, showboxes, portals, the rest. When you are done, [shop for a parcel](/shop).

## Hang art on the wall

Once you have a parcel, you can put actual chain art on it. We wrote that down here: [how to add art](https://www.voxels.com/art).

Short version: add an NFT image, copy the OpenSea URL (Ethereum, Polygon, or Base), paste it. CAIP-19 ids work too.

## Come back whenever

Build is in the menu. Art docs stay at [/art](/art). Neither one expires. If you forget everything except those two links, you can still build.
